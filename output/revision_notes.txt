IMPORTANT REVISION POINTS
- Cyber security refers to the practice and technologies used to protect computer systems, networks, programs and data from unauthorized access, damage or cyber attacks.
- One important concept in cyber security is the CIA TRIET.
- CIA stands for Confidentiality, Integrity and Availability.
- Cyber security involves protecting computer systems, networks, and information from digital threats.
- Human behaviour also plays an important role in maintaining security.
- Users should create strong passwords, keep software updated, avoid suspicious downloads and remain cautious when receiving unexpected messages.
